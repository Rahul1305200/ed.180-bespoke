Welcome ED-180 Patients

Dr. Jeffrey DeSarbo and the clinical team at ED-180 Treatment Programs have partnered with YouMind to bring you a non-invasive layer of physiological support.

Eating disorder recovery often involves periods of anticipatory anxiety and heightened nervous system stress. This autonomic friction can make traditional psychotherapy, psychiatric appointments, and supervised meal support feel particularly overwhelming.

YouMind Bespoke is designed to complement your current treatment pathway at ED-180 by providing a structured, at-home neuroacoustic protocol. By supporting nervous system down-regulation between your clinical appointments, YouMind helps you arrive at each session feeling more grounded, receptive, and regulated.

How to Get Started

How YouMind Bespoke Works

01

The 5-Minute Guided Vocal Check-In

Access the platform directly through your smartphone or web browser. There are zero apps to download, logins to remember, or long surveys to complete. You simply speak naturally in response to a series of brief conversational prompts.

02

Holistic Evaluation Across 5 Core Domains

As you speak, the system analyzes verbal and acoustic features to assess five foundational functional areas: Sleep, Mental Cognition, Emotional Regulation, Physical Energy, and Intention.

03

Individualized Neuroacoustic Protocol

From your assessment, YouMind builds a daily listening protocol tailored to your baseline. Using precisely specified temporal and frequency characteristics (isochronic tone stimulation), these sounds provide structured sensory support.

04

Nervous System Regulation

The auditory protocols offer a non-pharmacological tool to help safely ease your body out of sympathetic overdrive (“fight or flight”) and into a calmer, parasympathetic (“rest and digest”) state.
